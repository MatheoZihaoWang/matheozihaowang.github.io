## Bio

Zihao Wang develops stochastic generative learning methods for constrained generation, inverse problems, and decision making. His applications include medical imaging, multimodal AI systems, agents, and control.

He is an Assistant Professor in the Computer Science and Engineering Department at the University of Tennessee at Chattanooga and a Visiting Research Collaborator at the Athinoula A. Martinos Center for Biomedical Imaging at Massachusetts General Hospital and Harvard Medical School. Previously, he was a Research Fellow at the Martinos Center and a Research Engineer at Inria Sophia-Antipolis.
