Latent Visual Contrastive Reasoning with Multi-Branch Exploration by Kegeng Tang, Jingbo Wang, Yangming Zhang, Huanshu Zhang, Shaogang Ren, and Zihao Wang, was accepted as a **short paper** at the 2026 IEEE International Conference on Big Data. The reported acceptance rate is 19.2%.
