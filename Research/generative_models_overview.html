RESEARCH / GENERATIVE MODELS

From stochastic dynamics to reliable inference.

We study how generative models can use shared information, learned dynamics, and physical requirements to produce useful answers to scientific problems.

Overview Research questions SDE and BSDE Selected papers

Understanding the process of generation

A generative model learns statistical patterns in data and uses them to produce new examples. In a diffusion model, generation unfolds through many small steps: a noisy state is gradually transformed into a structured result. This evolving process offers opportunities to guide what the model produces.

Our research asks how that guidance can be grounded in the problem we want to solve. When translating a medical image, which anatomical information should remain? When moving between imaging modalities, how should the transformation unfold? When reconstructing an unknown object, how should the result agree with the measurements? These questions connect generative learning with statistical inference and scientific modeling.

INFORMATION

Preserve what different views share.

The same anatomy can look very different in two medical scans. Brightness may change while relationships between neighboring tissues still provide useful clues. We use local mutual information to bring these shared statistical relationships into the diffusion process, helping guide the changing image toward a target modality while retaining information from the input.

A model trained on target-modality images can then translate source modalities absent from training, where the local statistical relationships transfer sufficiently well. This also supports reuse of existing analysis tools, such as brain segmentation. [1]

DYNAMICS

Shape how the transformation unfolds.

Different parts of an image may require different changes. We develop diffusion dynamics in which image regions and channels can follow different learned transition schedules. This gives the model more flexibility to handle the relationships between a source observation and a target representation.

Learned from corresponding image pairs, these transitions support cross-modal generation across MRI, radar-to-optical translation, and industrial inspection. The work connects the learned transition to a continuous-time stochastic model and a sampler, with experiments demonstrating useful translations in fewer denoising steps. [2]

CONSTRAINTS

Work backward from the required outcome.

Scientific reconstruction begins with measurements. A CT image, for example, should reproduce the recorded X-ray projections when passed through an imaging model. We formulate terminal-conditioned inversion with backward stochastic differential equations, or BSDEs: an output requirement helps determine a suitable state inside a pretrained generative model.

The pretrained prior stays frozen. Reconstruction combines its learned image structure with the observation model. The BSDE formulation has existence and uniqueness guarantees under stated regularity assumptions; its numerical realization is demonstrated on sparse-view CT. [3]

Reading SDEs and BSDEs through random information

An SDE specifies a starting state and how drift and random shocks move it. A BSDE specifies a terminal random variable and a driver, then determines an adapted pair (Y, Z). Y is the value process; Z describes how new random information changes it.

The distinction concerns what is given and what must be solved. In both cases, information becomes available forward in time. Solving a BSDE backward does not give the process access to future noise.

A conditional-expectation view

For dX = dW and terminal rule Y(T) = X(T)^2, the zero-driver BSDE has the exact solution Y(t) = E[X(T)^2 | F(t)] = X(t)^2 + (T - t), with Z(t) = 2X(t). The term T - t accounts for noise that has not yet been revealed.

Z is also solved for. In this two-step example, Z(0) = 0; after the first step it is -2 on the X(1) = -1 branch and +2 on the X(1) = +1 branch.

This example explains the stochastic-analysis idea. The paper uses a score-induced driver and task-specific terminal specifications. Its BSDE is distinct from the reverse-time SDE used for diffusion sampling. [3]

Connecting the terminal requirement to a frozen prior

What “backward” means here

The associated BSDE is a terminal-value inference problem, distinct from the reverse-time SDE normally used for diffusion sampling. The theorem concerns the exact BSDE under its assumptions. The CT implementation uses a final measurement-discrepancy objective; it does not establish exact measurement agreement for every numerical reconstruction or generated sample.

研究方向 / 生成模型

从随机动力学，走向可靠推断。

我们研究如何将共享信息、学习到的动态规律与物理要求融入生成过程，让生成模型成为解决科学问题的工具。

研究概述 核心问题 SDE 与 BSDE 代表论文

理解并塑造生成的过程

生成模型从数据中学习统计规律，并据此产生新的样本。对于扩散模型，生成是一个逐步演化的过程：模型从带有噪声的状态出发，经过多次更新，逐渐形成有结构的结果。这一过程也为引导生成提供了切入点。

我们的研究关注：如何让这种引导与具体问题建立联系？转换医学影像时，哪些解剖信息应当保留？跨越不同成像模态时，变化应该如何发生？从有限观测重建未知对象时，结果又应当如何解释实际测量？这些问题将生成学习与统计推断、科学建模连接起来。

信息

在不同的外观中，保留共享信息。

同一解剖结构在两种医学扫描中，可能呈现完全不同的明暗对比。像素值会改变，组织之间的局部统计关系却仍能提供线索。我们利用局部互信息，将这些共享关系融入扩散生成过程，引导图像向目标模态转变，同时尽量保留来自输入的结构信息。

在局部统计关系能够充分迁移的条件下，仅使用目标模态图像训练的模型，就可以处理训练中未见过的源模态。这也使已有分析工具有机会服务于新的影像输入，例如将转换后的影像用于脑部结构分割。[1]

动力学

让不同区域，按照不同节奏变化。

图像的不同位置和通道，可能需要不同程度、不同速度的变化。我们将可学习的空间变化规律引入扩散动力学，使各个区域可以遵循不同的转换进程，从而更灵活地表达源观测与目标表示之间的关系。

这类转换规律由配对图像学习，并在 MRI、雷达到光学影像转换和工业检测中进行检验。我们将它与连续时间随机模型及数值采样器相联系，探索如何在保持结构对应关系的同时，减少生成所需的去噪步骤。[2]

约束

从结果必须满足的要求，反推生成状态。

科学重建从观测出发。例如，一幅 CT 重建图像经过成像模型计算后，应当能够解释实际记录的 X 射线投影。我们利用后向随机微分方程（BSDE），将输出端的要求写入终端条件，反向推断预训练生成模型内部的合适状态。

预训练先验保持冻结，重建过程将其学习到的图像规律与观测模型相结合。对于所构造的 BSDE，我们在给定正则性假设下建立解的存在性与唯一性，并通过稀疏视角 CT 展示相应的数值实现。[3]

从随机信息的角度理解 SDE 与 BSDE

SDE 给定初始状态，以及漂移和随机扰动如何推动状态变化。BSDE 给定终端随机变量与驱动项，再求出适应当前信息的一对过程 (Y, Z)：Y 表示随时间变化的值，Z 描述新到来的随机信息如何更新这个值。

区别在于给定什么、需要求解什么。两者的信息都随时间向前揭示；从终端条件反推，并不意味着过程可以提前知道未来噪声。

把当前值理解为条件期望

对于 dX = dW、终端关系 Y(T) = X(T)^2，零驱动 BSDE 的精确解为 Y(t) = E[X(T)^2 | F(t)] = X(t)^2 + (T - t)，且 Z(t) = 2X(t)。其中 T - t 表示尚未揭示的噪声对条件期望的贡献。

Z 也需要一并求出。这个两步示例中，Z(0) = 0；第一步之后，在 X(1) = -1 的分支上 Z 为 -2，在 X(1) = +1 的分支上 Z 为 +2。

这个示例用于解释随机分析思想。论文使用与 score 相关的驱动项和具体任务的终端条件；其中的 BSDE 与扩散采样使用的反向时间 SDE 是不同的数学构造。[3]
