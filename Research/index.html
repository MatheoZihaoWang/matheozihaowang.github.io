Research

From stochastic processes to useful AI systems.

My group develops mathematically grounded generative learning methods, then uses them to solve constrained, high-stakes problems in imaging, healthcare, agents, and control.

01

Generative models

Diffusion models, latent-variable learning, score matching, and stochastic flow inference.

02

Inverse problems

Physics-constrained generation and uncertainty-aware reconstruction for scientific imaging.

03

Healthcare AI

Cross-modality translation, image analysis, multimodal foundation models, and clinical decision support.
