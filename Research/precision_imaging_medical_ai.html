Research / Precision Imaging and Medical AI

What can we infer from a medical scan?

A CT scanner records X-ray measurements. Reconstructing an image, locating an anatomical boundary and describing a change since the last scan each require a different inference. We study how to check the evidence at each step.

Reconstruction

Which images agree with the measurements?

In sparse-view CT, the scanner measures the body from a limited set of angles. Several images can explain those projections while differing in details between the measured views. Our work on backward stochastic differential equations combines a frozen diffusion prior with the CT acquisition model. Projecting a candidate image back into measurement space lets us check its agreement with the recorded data.

Anatomy

Where is the boundary of the cochlea?

The cochlea is a small spiral structure whose boundaries can be difficult to resolve on clinical CT. We combine a model of cochlear shape with local image intensities to estimate a distribution over plausible boundaries. Its spread tells us where the scan constrains the segmentation tightly and where several contours remain plausible.

{% include medical_cochlea.html left="Visible edge" right="Plausible contours" caption="Schematic of one cochlear boundary. Follow the spiral outward. Where the edge is clear on the left, the three illustrative contours on the right overlap. Near the faint outer turn they fan apart. This local spread is what a single best contour hides and a Bayesian shape estimate can express." %} Bayesian cochlear segmentation · Medical Image Analysis

Imaging contrast

What should stay in place when contrast changes?

The same tissue can appear bright in one MRI sequence and dark in another. For image translation, the challenge is to change contrast while preserving the patient’s anatomy. Our mutual-information-guided diffusion method uses statistical relationships between local image regions to guide generation, including when the source modality was absent from training.

{% include medical_mri.html left="Source contrast" right="Target contrast" caption="Cross-modality translation schematic. Dot density represents signal intensity. Follow the two central cavities: their signal changes from sparse to dense while their outlines stay aligned. Local mutual information guides diffusion by comparing intensity patterns in corresponding neighborhoods, allowing a boundary to remain informative when its contrast reverses." %} Cross-modality image translation · IEEE TMI

Clinical interpretation

Can a model identify a finding and track how it changes?

In head CT after hypoxic ischemic injury, we studied whether a model could detect severe cerebral edema. Inspecting attention maps alongside correctly and incorrectly classified scans helped us examine which regions influenced its predictions.

Severe cerebral edema detection · Resuscitation

Reading two scans together

Follow-up imaging poses a different question. A report that correctly names a finding can still get its direction of change wrong. CT-ΔBench evaluates whether vision-language models can compare two 3D CT studies and describe interval changes. This makes temporal accuracy an explicit target for evaluation.

Longitudinal CT difference reporting · COLM 2026

研究方向 / 精准成像与医疗人工智能

一幅医学影像能告诉我们什么？

CT 扫描仪记录的是 X 射线测量值。从这些数据重建图像、勾画解剖边界，再到判断病灶与上次检查相比有何变化，每一步都涉及推断。我们研究如何在每一步检验推断所依据的证据。

图像重建

哪些图像与实测数据相符？

稀疏角度 CT 只从有限方向采集投影。多幅图像都可能解释这些测量，却在未采样方向的细节上有所不同。我们利用倒向随机微分方程，将冻结的扩散先验与 CT 采集模型结合。把候选图像重新投影到测量空间，就可以用实际记录的数据检验重建结果。

{% include medical_ct.html left="候选重建 A" right="候选重建 B" caption="双角度 CT 原理示意。每个填充方格代表一个单位的衰减。先逐行求和，再逐列求和：两幅图在这两个方向上的投影都是 (1, 1)，尽管内部结构不同。增加一个斜向投影就有机会区分它们。这说明重建既要接受实测投影的检验，也需要学习到的先验来约束测量尚不能确定的细节。" %} 稀疏角度 CT 重建 · ICML 2026

解剖结构

耳蜗的边界在哪里？

耳蜗细小且呈螺旋形，临床 CT 上的部分边界很难辨清。我们将耳蜗形状模型与局部图像灰度结合，估计一组可能轮廓的概率分布。分布集中的位置意味着图像对边界提供了较强约束；分布较宽的位置，则可能存在多种合理的勾画结果。

影像对比度

改变对比度时，哪些结构应当保留？

同一组织在一种 MRI 序列上可以呈高信号，在另一种序列上却呈低信号。跨模态转换需要改变组织对比度，同时保留这位患者的解剖结构。我们用局部图像区域之间的互信息引导扩散生成，并研究这种方法在源模态未参与训练时的表现。

{% include medical_mri.html left="源模态对比度" right="目标模态对比度" caption="跨模态转换原理示意。点越密表示信号越强。对照中央两处腔隙：同一位置由稀疏点变为密集点，轮廓仍能对齐。局部互信息比较对应邻域内的强度分布关系，即使边界两侧的明暗反转，仍能利用它们共有的结构信息引导扩散生成。" %} 跨模态医学图像转换 · IEEE TMI

临床解读

模型能否识别异常，并描述其变化？

在缺氧缺血性脑损伤后的头颅 CT 研究中，我们考察了模型识别重度脑水肿的能力。结合判断正确和错误的病例查看注意力图，可以分析哪些脑区影响了模型的预测。
