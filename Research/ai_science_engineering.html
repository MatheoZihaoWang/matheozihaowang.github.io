Research / AI meets Sci&Engineering

What can a model learn from the way a system is measured?

A telescope records the light around a star. A receiver samples a voltage waveform. In both cases, the quantity we want to know is only indirectly visible. Across our collaborations, the instrument, the physics and the design objective shape what we ask a model to learn and how we test its answer.

Astronomy

Finding a faint disk in the glare of its star

Reference stars help astronomers estimate how much of an image comes from the central star. Choosing useful references requires distinguishing their images from those containing circumstellar disks. For POLARIS, we used archival polarized-light observations from the SPHERE/IRDIS instrument to build a benchmark for that distinction. Statistical methods, generative models and vision-language models can then be compared on the same observations.

Electrical engineering

How much timing margin does a circuit leave?

Voltage fluctuations in a power distribution network can shift the timing of a digital signal. We used a genetic algorithm to search for decoupling capacitor configurations that control supply-induced jitter while keeping the component count low. At the receiver, we studied a complementary problem. A recurrent neural equalizer uses the history of a distorted waveform to recover the transmitted signal. The opening of the resulting eye diagram shows how much room the receiver has to distinguish bits in voltage and in time.

Nanophotonics

Which optical structure should we simulate next?

Changing the geometry of a metasurface changes its response to light. Testing many geometries with an electromagnetic solver can be expensive. In our review of language models for nanophotonics, we examine two roles for learning in this search. A surrogate estimates the spectrum of a proposed structure. An agent can write simulation code, call a solver and use its output to revise the design. The solver gives each proposal a physical test.

研究方向 / 人工智能与科学工程

从一个系统的测量方式中，模型能学到什么？

望远镜记录恒星周围的光，接收器对电压波形采样。两种情况下，观测量与我们真正想知道的量之间都隔着一个物理过程。在这些跨学科合作中，仪器能测到什么、系统遵循怎样的规律、设计需要满足哪些指标，共同决定了模型的学习任务和检验方法。

天文观测

怎样在明亮的恒星旁辨认暗弱的星周盘？

天文学家借助参考星估计中心恒星在图像中贡献了多少光。要选好参考图像，首先需要区分参考星图像与含有星周盘的图像。在 POLARIS 工作中，我们利用 SPHERE/IRDIS 仪器积累的偏振光观测建立了分类基准。统计方法、生成模型与视觉语言模型由此可以在同一批真实观测上进行比较。

电子工程

电路为正确判读一个比特留下了多少时序裕量？

配电网络中的电压波动会使数字信号的跳变时刻发生偏移。我们用遗传算法搜索去耦电容的配置，在控制电源诱发抖动的同时减少所需元件。在接收端，我们研究了另一个相关问题：如何利用失真波形的历史采样恢复发送信号？循环神经网络均衡器处理波形后，可以通过眼图开口的高度和宽度，分别评估接收器在电压和时间上判读比特的余量。

纳米光子学

下一个值得仿真的光学结构是什么？

改变超表面的几何结构，就会改变它对光的响应。用电磁求解器逐一检验大量候选结构，计算成本很高。我们在纳米光子学语言模型综述中梳理了两种用法：代理模型可以估计候选结构的光谱，智能体则可以编写仿真代码、调用求解器，并依据计算结果调整设计。这样，每次提出的新结构都可以接受物理仿真的检验。
