PhD Thesis

Research topic
Precision Image Reconstruction for Embodied Surgical Robot Navigation

This project studies precise, physically grounded image reconstruction for perception and navigation in embodied surgical robotic systems. The research connects inverse problems, generative modeling, uncertainty, and navigation-aware imaging.

Good preparation

A strong background in machine learning, computer vision, medical imaging, optimization, applied mathematics, robotics, or a closely related field.

How to express interest

Send a short research statement, your CV, and representative research or project work to zihao [.] wang@ieee.org.

Research topic
Agentic Industrial Defect Prediction

This project develops agentic learning systems for industrial defect prediction. The work focuses on combining predictive models, tool-using agents, and structured reasoning for reliable analysis of industrial data.

Good preparation

Experience with machine learning, data analysis, software development, or intelligent agents, together with an interest in applied industrial AI.
